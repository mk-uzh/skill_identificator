The project you will be looking at

A learning platform that universities run for their courses: assignments, quizzes, marking, grades. Hundreds of people have worked on it, and everything they did is public. You do not need to know the software, and nothing you are asked is technical.

What you will be doing

You will look at six of these developers and estimate, per competence, how far the records show they have worked in that area.

StepWhat happens
Task 1Six developers, one after another. For each one you read a short account of what the person actually did, and you set a level per competence, on your own.
OverviewEverything you entered, side by side. You can still correct it.
Task 2The same six people again, this time with a suggested level and its reasoning. You accept it, adjust it or keep your own answer.

About 25 minutes. You can return to any step using the links at the top right. Nothing is stored under your name.

The five levels

You judge what the records show about one competence, not the person. Each level assumes the one below it.

LevelIn shortWhat that looks like in the records
1Has touched it Small edits inside something that already existed. The approach was set by someone else.
2Works in it reliably Works reliably on ordinary tasks, accepted without rework.
3Solves real problems Finds the cause of a difficult fault, changes the existing structure instead of working around it, and explains the reasoning.
4Shapes the area Builds things other people then rely on, changes several parts of the software together, and regularly inspects other people's work here.
5Is the reference The person others turn to in this area. Sets the approach the project follows and corrects the direction of other people's work.
n/oNot observable These records show nothing about this competence. Use it freely, it is information rather than a gap in your judgement.

The scale follows the European e-Competence Framework, EN 16234-1.

What the records show

Selected competence: develop automated software tests

A typical piece of this person's work. A teacher reported that publishing a quiz failed with an error whenever a marking scheme was attached to it. DEV-07 traced the problem back to a change somebody else had made months earlier, explained in the task discussion why that change collided with the way a quiz is locked while it is being published, removed the part that caused it, and added a test that reproduces the whole situation so that it cannot come back unnoticed. Work of this shape, finding the cause rather than covering the symptom, appears repeatedly across the two years.

  • 18 of 148 changes touched tests; in 11 the tests were new, covering behaviour nobody had checked before.
  • Wrote the test for a new calendar search: it opens the page, types a term, checks the right entries appear. AI assistance declared
  • Checked the test code of seven other people, asking twice for rework.
  • None of their own test changes were sent back.
